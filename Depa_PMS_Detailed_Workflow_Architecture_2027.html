1. Strategy cascade and form assignment

Framework architecture - one cycle, five forms
Group strategy and annual OKRsExecution Excellence; Disciplined Growth; Priority Markets; Shareholder Value; Institutional Readiness
→
GCEO sets N-1 prioritiesApproved objectives, measures, targets and annual weightings for Group leadership
→
KBU Heads cascade N-2 / N-3Translate Group priorities into KBU, function, project and role accountability
→
Population and form assignmentGrade, entity, job family and role type select one governed form for the cycle
Form 1 - DUG leadershipGrade 18+ - Group leadership
  • Customer
  • Financials
  • People & Culture
  • Strategic Initiatives
Form 2 - KBU leadershipGrade 18+ - operating business
  • Business Development
  • Backlog & New Awards
  • Projects and Financials
  • Optional Strategic Initiatives
Form 3 - Department headsGrade 17 and below - KPI based
  • 4-6 meaningful KPIs
  • Functional / team outcomes
  • Mandatory Group / KBU linkage
  • Weights total 100%
Form 4 - Project / professionalGrade 17 and below - KPI based
  • Delivery and milestones
  • Cost / productivity and quality
  • Customer / stakeholder
  • Technical / functional output
Form 5 - Admin / supportGrade 17 and below - role standards
  • Core responsibilities
  • Quality, timeliness and service
  • Process / compliance
  • Collaboration / improvement
Common record across forms: objective / key result or role standard - strategic link - measure - baseline - target - weight - actual - employee comment - manager rating - manager justification - evidence - development action.
Population gate
Grade and role type map to exactly one form.
Line-of-sight gate
Leadership and KPI forms identify the supported Group / KBU objective.
Design gate
4-6 meaningful outcomes; no artificial activity checklist.
Weight gate
Weighted forms must equal exactly 100% before submission.

3. Return, correction and controlled-change loops

Every non-standard route requires reason, notification and audit event
Goal return loop
  • Manager returns with mandatory reason
  • System reopens only goal-setting fields
  • Employee revises and resubmits
  • Old submission remains in history
Goal approval -> Goal setting -> Goal approval
Evidence correction loop
  • Evidence marked Rejected / Clarification
  • Employee adds replacement or explanation
  • Original evidence is retained
  • Manager or P&C re-verifies
Assessment -> controlled reopen -> verification
Material objective change
  • Request explains changed business condition
  • Impact on cascaded goals identified
  • Manager and required authority approve
  • Old baseline / target / weight superseded, never deleted
Active plan -> approval -> new version
P&C quality return
  • Return identifies failed rule
  • Manager corrects rating, comment or evidence
  • System reruns quality checks
  • P&C releases only after pass
Quality gate -> Manager assessment -> Quality gate
Calibration adjustment
  • Panel proposes calibrated rating
  • Adjustment reason is mandatory
  • Original manager rating remains visible
  • P&C confirms and locks final result
Calibration -> P&C confirmation -> Final

5. Governance rules, data entities and event trail

Framework controls plus recommended system enforcement
Strategy cascadeGrade 18+ objectives originate from approved Group / KBU priorities; downstream KPIs retain line of sight.
Quality over volumeKPI forms use 4-6 meaningful outcomes; activities are not used unless they represent a genuine outcome or standard.
Weight integrityEvery weighted form equals exactly 100% before employee submission and manager approval.
Controlled changeApproved objectives are versioned and locked; material change needs authorization and cannot be made retrospectively at year-end.
Evidence standardRatings above expectations require clear evidence and manager justification; evidence verification state is visible.
Rating philosophyOne five-point scale applies across all forms; final judgment reflects outcome, evidence, context and calibration.
No forced curveCalibration challenges inconsistency but does not mechanically impose a bell-curve quota.
Segregation of dutiesEmployees cannot approve their own plan; managers cannot finalize calibration; P&C controls release and close.
Audit completenessEvery submit, approve, return, change, verification, override and finalization records actor, timestamp, reason and before / after state.
